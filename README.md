# 🏨 Kayak Scraper

Kayak Scraper is a **free and open-source** scraper that gets you **unlimited** detailed Kayak data for free.

## ✨ What Can I Get?

- 🏨 **Full details on 2M+ hotels & stays** — stars, rating, address, phone, amenities, photos, policies & FAQs
- 💰 **Room rates from hundreds of booking sites** — nightly & total price per site, room type, beds & the cheapest deal
- ⭐ **Millions of verified guest reviews** — score, full text, pros & cons, source site & an AI summary
- 📉 **12 months of price history** — cheapest time to book, cheapest month & weekday, seasons & nearby deals

## 📚 Endpoints

6 endpoints cover everything you need.

| Endpoint | Path | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| Hotel Details | `/hotels/details` | Stars, rating, amenities, photos, policies, review summary and FAQs |
| Search Locations | `/locations/search` | Cities, airports, landmarks and hotels with their KAYAK IDs |
| Hotel Rates | `/hotels/rates` | Room prices on every booking site KAYAK lists for the hotel |
| Hotel Reviews | `/hotels/reviews` | Guest reviews with score, text, pros and cons, 10 per page |
| Hotel Price Insights | `/hotels/price-insights` | Cheapest time to book, cheapest month and weekday, seasons |
| Nearby Hotels | `/hotels/nearby` | Closest, cheapest and most recommended hotels around it |
